What Is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a calculation based on weight and height. For most adults, it can be used as a general screening tool, but it does not directly measure body fat or overall health.

Adult BMI Categories

  • Below 18.5: Underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9: Healthy weight range
  • 25 to 29.9: Overweight
  • 30 or above: Obesity range

These categories are general adult screening ranges. BMI should not be used alone to diagnose a health condition, and different guidance may apply to children and teenagers.
