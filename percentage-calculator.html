How to Calculate Percentages

1. What percentage is one number of another?
Divide the first number by the total and multiply by 100.

Example: 25 out of 200 = 12.5%.

2. How do you find a percentage of a number?
Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100.

Example: 15% of 500 = 75.

3. How do you calculate percentage change?
Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, and multiply by 100.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I calculate 10% of a number?

Multiply the number by 10 and divide by 100. For example, 10% of 500 is 50.

2. How do I calculate a discount percentage?

Subtract the discounted price from the original price, divide the result by the original price, and multiply by 100.
