About AllToolsHub

Welcome to AllToolsHub, a website that provides simple and free online tools to help with everyday calculations and tasks.

Our Tools

Our website currently offers six free tools: Percentage Calculator, Age Calculator, BMI Calculator, Word Counter, QR Code Generator, and Password Generator.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make useful online tools easy to access and simple to use. We aim to improve our website and add more helpful tools over time.

Accuracy and Responsibility

We try to make our tools useful and accurate. However, results may not always be suitable for every situation. Please verify important calculations independently. The BMI calculator provides general information and is not a substitute for medical advice.
